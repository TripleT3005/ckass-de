Hosting and server log files

The website is hosted by STRATO GmbH, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with STRATO.

With every request, your browser technically has to send data to the server, which STRATO stores in log files:

  • IP address (anonymized by STRATO in the log files),
  • date and time of the request,
  • requested page or file, status code and amount of data transferred,
  • the previously visited page (referrer), if your browser sends it,
  • browser and operating system (user agent) and preferred languages.

This data is used to deliver the website, to keep it secure and stable, and for aggregated access statistics. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a secure and working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Log files are kept for at most six weeks and then deleted. I do not combine them with other data. Details are in STRATO's privacy policy.

The connection is encrypted via HTTPS.

Language selection

When you open an address without a language, the server redirects you to the German or English version based on your browser's language setting (Accept-Language). Nothing is stored, and the flags at the top right are ordinary links.

Contact by email

If you email me, I process your address and the content in order to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I delete the message once it has been dealt with, unless legal obligations require otherwise.

Links to other websites

The website contains links to other websites, for example to GitHub for source code and downloads. Only when you click such a link does your browser connect to the other website; its own privacy policy then applies.

Privacy information about the programs themselves is on each project page.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You may object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21). To do so, contact me at the address above.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one where you live.
